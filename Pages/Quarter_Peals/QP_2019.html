Tom Freeston has been an active ringer since the late 1940s, and rang his first quarter peal at Boston Stump in 1948. Tom is a much cherished ringer and teacher in the Boston area, and supporter of Sunday morning ringing every week. Tom has taught many local ringers, including current Butterwick ringers under the age of 20.
John Collett is much loved former resident member of the LDG, teacher and conductor and fund raiser. John is especially noted for his support of the annual Race Night fundraiser, and for encouraging the resurrection of a range of Eastern Branch events. John rang his first quarter peal in 1946, at Burford.
Between them, John and Tom have made and continue to make an incalculable contribution to the teaching and continuation of church bell ringing; as well as being excellent company in and out of the ringing room.
From all of us, many thanks!
