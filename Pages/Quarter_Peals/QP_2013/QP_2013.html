Thursday 31st January

St Andrew, Butterwick (9-3-12)

1260 St Clement’s College Bob Minor (being 720 & 540) in 43 mins

  1. Rhoda Reynolds
  2. Kate Morgan
  3. G John Collett
  4. Thomas J Freeston
  5. Philip H Dawson
  6. Christopher CP Woodcock (C)
Specially arranged and rung to congratulate Tom Freeston on being given a Civic Award for “Service to the Community” by Boston Borough Council. This was awarded for his long and loyal service as Bell Ringing Captain at St Botolph’s (Boston Stump) and for services to bell ringing across the district. The award will be presented at a special ceremony on 18th April.
Tom has been Bell Ringing Captain at Boston Stump for 41 years, standing down at the end of last year, and has been ringing at the Stump for 66 years. He has also been President of the Eastern Branch of the Lincoln Diocesan Guild standing down last week at the AGM. Congratulations Tom, you deserve it, especially for climbing the 209 steps up the Stump each week!
Rung also to congratulate Tom Palmer, local ringer on the birth of his third great grandchild in the early hours of this morning. The band wish to associate Tom Palmer with this quarter (noting that Tom sat in the tower throughout the quarter peal).
