Welcome to our Website!

We hope that you find this site to be useful and informative. If you have any comments or suggestions about the content, feel free to contact us. In common with many rural areas, there are more bells to be rung than there are ringers to ring them, but we do our best to ensure that any requests for help are dealt with. The East Coast of Lincolnshire is a popular tourist area, and if you have visited before, you will know that the County is not completely flat! We will give you a warm welcome whether you are on holiday, or are arranging an outing with your own band, and will be pleased to help you find a practice night to enjoy

Race-Night Fund-Raising Event - Alford - 25 Oct 25

The Eastern Branch Guild Fundraiser was a Race Night held at Alford church hall.

There was a full race card of 9 races, each race having 8 horses. The last race of the night was the 'Betty Collett Cup'. This was a race-off between the winning horses from the previous eight races. The winner of this race was 'Susan Constant', owned by Ian Evans.

Overall, including the raffle, we raised over £817.70 for the Guild's Training and Recruitment Fund.

Heritage Open Days 2026

Heritage Open Days 2026 will be 11-20 September.

Heritage Open Days provide a fantastic opportunity for churches across the country to open their doors, welcome their communities, and share the remarkable story of church bellringing. Every year, thousands of visitors discover the history, heritage and people behind our towers, many stepping inside a ringing chamber for the very first time.

To help individual towers make the most of this opportunity, the Central Council of Church Bell Ringers has created the Heritage Open Days Publicity Toolkit.
